Reading it

The loops are flat

Coverage grows by exactly eight seeds a loop and seeds are reached at the base rate throughout, so a hotkey placed at loop 10 is worth what one at loop 1 is worth. Nothing saturates by loop 11 — running deeper keeps paying at the same rate.

Clean sets only on the newest nine

The floor needs a real submission to enumerate, so one build ran per task on the newest tasks, at that task’s first hit. Three of eighteen eligible off-band seeds came back clean — worth about +0.043 on the round, against the 0.388 a lone band hit scores.

Why this arm

Of six arms swept, wide/k9 never built at all — HEK293’s band-formation wall drops from 9 to 8 once the cut is 900. Narrow k=8 keeps a 2.75× pool margin where wide k=8 holds 1.61×, and carries more absolute clean seeds at matched depth.
